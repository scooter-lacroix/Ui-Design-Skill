# Organic / Natural — Token Card

## Identity
- **Mood**: Peaceful, handcrafted, grounded
- **Best For**: Wellness brands, sustainable products, food/beverage, eco-conscious companies, yoga/meditation apps
- **Avoid For**: Tech/SaaS platforms, fintech, gaming, high-energy marketing sites

## Color Tokens
| Token | Value | Usage |
|:------|:------|:------|
| Background | `#FDFCF8` | Off-white rice paper |
| Foreground | `#2C2C24` | Deep loam/charcoal text |
| Primary | `#5D7052` | Moss green — CTAs, icons, links |
| Secondary | `#C18C5D` | Terracotta/clay — outlines, accents |
| Accent | `#E6DCCD` | Sand/beige — subtle fills |
| Muted | `#F0EBE5` | Stone — backgrounds |
| Border | `#DED8CF` | Raw timber — card borders |

## Typography
- **Heading Font**: Fraunces (variable serif)
- **Body Font**: Nunito or Quicksand (rounded sans)
- **Key Weights**: 400, 600, 700, 800
- **Display Scale**: text-7xl (responsive from text-5xl)

## Geometry
- **Border Radius**: 16-24px standard; organic blob shapes via `border-radius: 60% 40% 30% 70% / 60% 30% 70% 40%`
- **Border Style**: Soft timber borders at 50% opacity; sometimes double or thick white borders
- **Shadow System**: Soft, color-tinted shadows — moss green (`rgba(93,112,82,0.15)`) and clay tinted, never pure black

## Motion
- **Easing**: ease (natural feel)
- **Speed**: 300-700ms (gentle, unhurried)
- **Signature Motion**: Elements lift like picking up a river stone; `hover:scale-105` with deepened colored shadow

## Signature Elements (Bold Factor)
- Blob backgrounds with `blur-3xl` ambient color washes
- Rotated image frames (-2deg with thick white border)
- Asymmetric card border radii cycling through 6 patterns
- Curved dashed SVG connectors (hand-drawn look)
- Global grain/noise texture overlay at 3-4% opacity with multiply blend
- Hover micro-rotations on testimonial cards (`hover:rotate-1`)

## Anti-Patterns
- Sharp 90-degree angles (there are no straight lines in nature)
- Pure black shadows
- Mechanical uniform grids (use staggered, varied layouts)
- Harsh snap transitions
- Flat unshadowed elements
- Neon or highly saturated colors

## Blend Compatibility
- **Family**: organic
- **Blends Well With**: neumorphism, botanical-organic, luxury
- **Conflicts With**: terminal, retro/90s, cyberpunk
