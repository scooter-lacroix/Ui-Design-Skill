# Serif (Business) — Token Card

## Identity
- **Mood**: Editorial, timeless, refined
- **Best For**: Premium brands, law firms, consultancies, editorial publications, architecture studios, literary journals, luxury hospitality
- **Avoid For**: Youth-oriented products, playful brands, tech startups, gaming, casual social apps

## Color Tokens
| Token | Value | Usage |
|:------|:------|:------|
| `background` | `#FAFAF8` | Primary canvas (warm ivory) |
| `foreground` | `#1A1A1A` | Primary text (rich black) |
| `muted` | `#F5F3F0` | Secondary surfaces, card backgrounds |
| `muted-foreground` | `#6B6B6B` | Secondary text (warm gray) |
| `accent` | `#B8860B` | Links, highlights, interactive elements (burnished gold) |
| `accent-secondary` | `#D4A84B` | Gradients and hover states (lighter gold) |
| `border` | `#E8E4DF` | Rules, dividers, card borders |
| `card` | `#FFFFFF` | Card surfaces (pure white for lift from ivory) |

## Typography
- **Heading Font**: Playfair Display (high-contrast serif — the soul of the design)
- **Body Font**: Source Sans 3 (clean, readable sans-serif)
- **Key Weights**: Normal (headings), Semibold (card titles), Medium (labels), 400/500 (body)
- **Display Scale**: text-7xl / 4.5rem hero headline; large display numbers at text-5xl+

## Geometry
- **Border Radius**: Conservative — buttons `rounded-md` (6px), cards `rounded-lg` (8px)
- **Border Style**: 1px solid thin rules in warm gray (#E8E4DF); accent gold rules for highlighted cards
- **Shadow System**: Extremely subtle — shadow-sm (0.04 opacity) to shadow-lg (0.08 opacity) using rich black tint; refinement over depth

## Motion
- **Easing**: ease-out (gentle, inevitable)
- **Speed**: 150ms (subtle) to 200ms (standard)
- **Signature Motion**: Restrained refinement — brightness shifts of 5-10%, shadow enhancement on hover, underlines appearing; no translate/lift effects

## Signature Elements (Bold Factor)
- Playfair Display as the non-negotiable heading font at oversized scale (7xl hero) — commands through beauty, not volume
- Thin horizontal rule line system creating editorial rhythm as section dividers and card top accents
- Small caps section labels using IBM Plex Mono with 0.15em tracking in uppercase
- Burnished gold (#B8860B) as the single accent color — inspired by gold leaf and gilded book edges
- Generous whitespace with py-32 to py-44 section padding — the design breathes
- Large serif display numbers for stats and pricing at dramatic sizes

## Anti-Patterns
- Trendy translate/lift effects on hover — too ephemeral for timeless aesthetic
- Bouncy, overshooting, or spring-based animations — motion must feel inevitable
- Multiple accent colors — the single gold accent is the entire color distinction
- Sans-serif headings — Playfair Display is mandatory for all headlines
- Cramped layouts or tight spacing — whitespace is an active design element
- Cold or stark gray palettes — warmth is non-negotiable (ivory, not white; warm gray, not neutral gray)

## Blend Compatibility
- **Family**: luxury
- **Blends Well With**: Botanical / Organic Serif, Swiss Minimalist, Simple Dark
- **Conflicts With**: Playful Geometric, Kinetic, Terminal
