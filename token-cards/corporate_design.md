# Corporate Trust — Token Card

## Identity
- **Mood**: Professional, Vibrant, Dimensional
- **Best For**: Enterprise SaaS, fintech, B2B platforms, startup marketing pages, professional dashboards
- **Avoid For**: Edgy consumer apps, luxury brands, retro/vintage products, experimental portfolios

## Color Tokens
| Token | Value | Usage |
|:------|:------|:------|
| Background | `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) | Page base — subtle cool grey/white |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` (White) | Cards and raised elements |
| Primary | `#4F46E5` (Indigo 600) | Core brand color, buttons, gradients |
| Secondary | `#7C3AED` (Violet 600) | Gradient endpoints, accents |
| Text Main | `#0F172A` (Slate 900) | High-contrast body and headings |
| Text Muted | `#64748B` (Slate 500) | Supporting and secondary text |
| Accent/Success | `#10B981` (Emerald 500) | Positive indicators and status |
| Border | `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) | Subtle card and input separation |

## Typography
- **Heading Font**: Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Body Font**: Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Key Weights**: ExtraBold (800), Bold (700), SemiBold (600), Medium (500), Regular (400)
- **Display Scale**: `text-6xl` (60px) on desktop; `text-4xl` on mobile; Major Third (1.250) scale

## Geometry
- **Border Radius**: `rounded-xl` (12px) for cards, `rounded-lg` (8px) for inputs, `rounded-full` for primary buttons
- **Border Style**: Thin 1px borders using `#E2E8F0`; focus rings via `ring-2 ring-indigo-500`
- **Shadow System**: Colored blue-tinted shadows (indigo-based rgba) with layered multi-shadow hover states; blur orbs at 3xl for atmospheric depth

## Motion
- **Easing**: `ease-out` for natural deceleration
- **Speed**: `duration-200` for general interactions; `duration-500` for complex animations and image zooms
- **Signature Motion**: Card lift (`-translate-y-1`) combined with shadow intensification on hover; gentle `animate-pulse` on floating decorative orbs

## Signature Elements (Bold Factor)
- Colored shadows with blue/purple tints (`rgba(79, 70, 229, ...)`) replacing neutral gray shadows
- Isometric 3D transforms on hero cards (`rotate-x-[5deg] rotate-y-[-12deg]`) and alternating feature cards
- Indigo-to-violet gradient text on split headlines (first words solid, trailing words gradient)
- Elevated card system with lift hover and multi-layer shadow deepening
- Large blurred gradient orbs (400-600px, `blur-3xl`) positioned absolutely for atmospheric depth
- Micro-interactions: arrow `translate-x-1` on hover, chevron `rotate-180` on accordion open, image `scale-105` zoom

## Anti-Patterns
- Cold, sterile corporate layouts without color energy or personality
- Flat design with no depth — dimensional shadows and transforms are essential
- Neutral gray drop shadows without brand-tinted coloring
- Cluttered or cramped spacing — generous white space between sections is mandatory
- Harsh or jarring animations — motion must be smooth and professional, never abrupt
- Monochromatic palettes that ignore the indigo-violet gradient spectrum

## Blend Compatibility
- **Family**: systematic
- **Blends Well With**: SaaS Design, Swiss Minimalist, Material Design
- **Conflicts With**: Neo-brutalism, Vaporwave, Art Deco
